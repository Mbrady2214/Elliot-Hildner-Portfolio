01

Brief

Rain is part of the game.

Wet gloves

Cold, waterlogged hands lose grip and feel, which hurts every shot.

Soaked thighs

Rain runs off the torso and dumps onto the legs, adding weight and discomfort.

Restricted mobility

Rain jackets stiffen or cling when wet and limit rotation and follow-through.

At a glanceGoal · Approach · Result

Goal

Keep the golfer's hands and thighs dry without costing any swing.

Approach

Foam channels inspired by leaf gutters to steer water off the body, plus over-mitt sleeves for the glove hand.

Result

Three prototypes and a finished jacket. Heat-pressed foam channels made the final build after testing against printed TPU strips.

02

Research

The market, the jobs, the trade-offs.

  1. Keep grip and hand function reliable in the wet, and keep the swing completely free.
  2. Stop moisture building up on the forearms and sleeves, and stop rain running onto the legs.
  3. Nature sheds and steers water with surface geometry, micro-textures and directional structures: leaf gutters, feathers, crocodile scales.
03
