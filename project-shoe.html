01

Brief

Heritage footwear, pointed at a market nobody designs for.

PF Flyers' canvas Grounder was my starting point and beekeeping was the market. Beekeepers care about sustainability, and their biggest problem with boots is simple: bees get inside them.

At a glanceGoal · Approach · Result

Goal

Keep bees out of the boot without making it hot, heavy or only good at the hive.

Approach

A Chelsea boot in organic cotton canvas, a cinch strap that seals the collar, and a 3D-printed TPU sole modeled to a scan of the upper.

Result

A wearable prototype, an 11-page tech pack and an estimated 6.7 kg CO2e per pair, less than half of a leather Chelsea boot.

02

Research

What beekeepers wear today, and where it falls short.

  1. Every boot I looked at leaves the collar open, and that is exactly where bees get in.
  2. None of them lead with sustainability, which is the beekeeper's whole value system.
  3. Comfortable slip-on boots already exist. They just aren't sealed or built for the hive.
