Brief

Vintage look, modern build.

Flat track is raced on dirt ovals, turning left lap after lap. I wanted the shape of an early racing goggle built with the things BOBSTER already does well: TPU frames, foam padding and a simple black finish, the same cues as their Cruiser 3 and Ballast goggles.

At a glanceGoal · Approach · Result

Goal

Keep the 1930s look and add modern comfort and better side vision.

Approach

A TPU frame with a translucent temple shield, a foam face cushion and an adjustable strap, built in four rounds.

Result

A wearable 3D-printed TPU goggle in BOBSTER's simple black.
