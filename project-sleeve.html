Brief

Recovery gear that knows where the stress is.

Pitchers put the ulnar collateral ligament (UCL) through repetitive, high-velocity stress. That creates microtrauma that needs active recovery to head off a major injury. Compression and temperature therapy are both proven ways to reduce inflammation and support recovery after that kind of stress, but nothing I found was built around the UCL itself. Roughly 35% of MLB pitchers have had UCL reconstruction surgery.

At a glanceGoal · Approach · Result

Goal

Help pitchers recover between outings by targeting the UCL instead of the whole arm.

Approach

Graduated compression, a UCL support strap and a pocket for heat or ice, worked out over 50+ sketches.

Result

Seven prototypes and a final sleeve in 87% nylon / 13% spandex with a non-slip silicone liner.

Final Design

Built for the hours after the outing.

  • UCL support band for adjustable, targeted compression along the UCL pathway.
  • Graduated pressure levels to promote circulation and faster recovery.
  • A pocket along the UCL for personalized post-throw heat or ice.
  • Non-slip silicone liner so the sleeve stays put instead of sliding down the arm.
